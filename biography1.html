Dr. B. R. Ambedkar (1891-1956)

Father of the Indian Constitution

Full Name: Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar
Born: 14 April 1891, Mhow, Madhya Pradesh
Parents: Ramji Maloji Sakpal & Bhimabai Sakpal
Wives: Ramabai Ambedkar, Dr. Savita Ambedkar
Education: BA - Elphinstone College, MA & PhD - Columbia University, MSc & DSc - London School of Economics, Barrister-at-Law - Gray's Inn

Early Life & Discrimination

Born in a Mahar (Dalit) family which was considered untouchable at that time. As a child he faced severe caste discrimination in school - was made to sit outside the classroom and not allowed to touch water taps. Despite this humiliation, his father encouraged education. His brilliance was noticed by Sayajirao Gaekwad III, Maharaja of Baroda, who gave him a scholarship.

Education - Most Highly Educated Indian

Ambedkar remains one of the most qualified Indians in history. He earned doctorates from Columbia University (USA) and London School of Economics. He studied economics, law, sociology and politics. He was the first Dalit to go to college in India and became a world-class economist and lawyer.

Journey & Major Movements

1924 - Bahishkrit Hitakarini Sabha: Formed to promote education and welfare of depressed classes.

1927 - Mahad Satyagraha: Led thousands of Dalits to Chavdar Lake in Mahad, Maharashtra to assert the right to drink water. He also burnt Manusmriti for justifying caste discrimination.

1930 - Kalaram Temple Entry Movement: Led protest for Dalits' right to enter Hindu temples in Nashik.

1932 - Poona Pact: Disagreement with Gandhiji on separate electorate for depressed classes. Signed Poona Pact which gave reserved seats instead of separate electorate.

1936 - Independent Labour Party & Scheduled Castes Federation: Formed political parties to fight for labour rights and Dalit rights.

1942 - As Labour Member: As Member of Viceroy's Executive Council, he brought 8-hour working day, minimum wages, and labour reforms in India.

Father of Indian Constitution

After Independence, he was appointed as Chairman of the Drafting Committee on 29 Aug 1947. He took 2 years, 11 months, 18 days to draft the world's largest constitution. He included fundamental rights, equality, abolition of untouchability (Article 17), and reservation for upliftment of SC/ST. He served as India's First Law Minister (1947-51).

Philosophy & Principles

1. Equality: Every human is equal regardless of birth
2. Liberty, Equality, Fraternity: Inspired by Buddhism and French Revolution
3. Annihilation of Caste: Caste system must be destroyed to create a just society
4. Educate, Agitate, Organize: His famous slogan for empowerment

Books & Achievement

Wrote "Annihilation of Caste", "Who Were the Shudras?", "The Buddha and His Dhamma" and "The Problem of the Rupee". He was awarded Bharat Ratna in 1990 (posthumously). He converted to Buddhism on 14 Oct 1956 in Nagpur with 5 lakh followers - this is known as Dhamma Chakra Pravartan Din.

Death & Legacy

Passed away on 6 Dec 1956 in Delhi. His cremation place is called Chaitya Bhoomi in Mumbai. His death anniversary is observed as Mahaparinirvan Diwas. His birthday 14 April is celebrated as Ambedkar Jayanti and Equality Day. He is an inspiration for social justice movements worldwide.

"I like the religion that teaches liberty, equality and fraternity."
- Dr. B. R. Ambedkar
