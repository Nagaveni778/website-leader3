About Indian Leaders

Indian Leaders - Vision • Courage • Nation logo shows 7 great leaders with India map tricolor in center representing unity in diversity.

Why We Created This Website?

1. To Inspire Youth: Today youth spends hours on social media but don't know real heroes. We saw students don't know who is Kittur Rani Chennamma who defeated British 33 years before Rani Lakshmibai, or Savitribai Phule who opened first girls school when people threw cow dung. This website brings their real sacrifice story to inspire.

2. Educational Purpose: For school/college projects students search Google but get only 2-3 lines. Our site gives full section-wise history - Early Life, Struggle, Major Reforms, Death & Legacy, Famous Quotes with dates (like Gandhi's Dandi March 385km 12 March 1930, Bhagat Singh hanged 23 March 1931 7:23PM). Helps in exams, speeches, essays.

3. Preserve Forgotten History: Books only teach 2-3 leaders. We included 10 leaders from different fields - Freedom Fighters (Gandhi, Bose, Bhagat Singh, Lakshmibai, Chennamma), Constitution Maker (Ambedkar), Missile Man (Kalam), Women Reformers (Savitribai, Sarojini Naidu), Strong PM (Indira Gandhi). So no hero is forgotten.

4. Tricolor Theme & Design: Our design matches Indian flag - Saffron header (#ff3c11) for courage, White body for truth, Green footer (#138808) for growth - like our logo with orange-green border. Single page for all 10 leaders so user scrolls easily without opening 10 pages.

5. Made by Student for Students: This is a college project made in C:/Users/dell/Desktop/Leader/ folder. Not political, no party promotion. Only aim is Inspiring Leaders, Inspiring Nation as written in header. All photos from Wikimedia Commons public domain, info from authentic sources.

Our Goal:

If even one student after reading Rani Lakshmibai's jump from fort with child on back, or Kalam selling newspapers to become President, feels motivated to work for nation - our website purpose is fulfilled. Jai Hind!
