Famous Leaders of India

Mahatma Gandhi

Mahatma Gandhi was a great leader of India's freedom movement and promoted truth and non-violence.

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar was a social reformer, jurist and an important architect of the Indian Constitution.

Bhagat Singh

Bhagat Singh was an indian anti-colonial revolutionary participated in the mistaken murder of a junior british police officer in december 1928 in what was intended to be retaliation for the death of an indian nationalist.

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was a renowned scientist and former President of India.

Subhas Chandra Bose

Subhas Chandra Bose was a prominent freedom fighter and leader in India's independence struggle.

Savitribai Phule

Savitribai Phule was an one of the india's first female teachers and a major social reformer. She worked for womens education,equality and the rights of oppressed communities.

Rani lakshmi bai

Ranilakshmi bai also known as jhansi ki rani, was one of the most famous indian freedom fighters. She is remeber for her courage,leadership and resistence against british rule.

Sarojini naidu

Sarogini naidu was a famous indian freedom fighter,poet and political leader. She is popularly known as the "Nightingale of india" because of a beautiful poetry.

Kittur Rani chennamma

Kittur Rani chennamma was a brave queen of Kittur in Karnataka she is remebered for her courageous fight against the british east india company.

Indira Gandhi

Indira Gandhi was a important indian political leader and the first and, to date, only women to serve as prime minister of india she served as prime minister from 1966-1977 and again from 1980-1984.
